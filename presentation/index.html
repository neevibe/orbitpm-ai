⚠️ The Operational Reality

  • ✕
    Excel Trackers: 8 departments maintaining separate .xlsx files. No single source of truth. Conflicting versions circulated via email.
  • ✕
    Manual Status Reporting: PMs spent hours each week filling cells row by row — reporting on work instead of doing it.
  • ✕
    Static Dashboards: PowerPoint snapshots already outdated by the time they were presented to leadership.
  • ✕
    Siloed Dependencies: Cross-functional dependencies between Engineering and Digital were invisible to each other.
  • ✕
    Meeting-Heavy Coordination: Back-to-back meetings because there was no shared real-time view of project status.

💡 Why Traditional Tools Failed

Jira / Asana / Monday.com — Built for agile software teams, not airport infrastructure PMOs. No governance workflows, no CCO Decision Matrices.

SAP / Oracle PPM — 6-12 month implementation cycles and massive licensing costs. Unacceptable for a fast-moving commercial team.

Power BI / Tableau — Visualization layers only. They display data but cannot manage projects, track risks, or automate reporting.

⚠️ Risk Register

3×3 Impact × Likelihood heatmap. 12 risk categories. Live score calculator. Open/Closed toggle. Undo history stack. Two-step creation: Dept → Project → Risk details.

📄 Excel Import / Export

Bi-directional compatibility. Smart parser with fuzzy header matching. Multi-sheet export: Dashboard, Master Index, Risk Register, per-department sheets. Zero workflow disruption.

🎯 Department Intelligence

Overview grid with KPI cards per department. Click any department → deep-dive into all projects with inline editing across 14+ fields (status, priority, progress, KPI, dependencies, support team, etc.).

🤖 AI Insights & Assistant

AI Insights Engine: One-click portfolio analysis. Health scores, department grades (A-F), actionable recommendations.

AI Chat Assistant: Natural language queries — "Show me delayed projects in Engineering" — instant, formatted answers.

👥 Team & Workload

Dynamic resource aggregation. Per-owner project counts. Overload detection: 🔴 15+ | 🟡 10-14 | 🟢 <10 projects. Prevents burnout, ensures balanced allocation.

📊 Analytics Suite

5 interactive Recharts visualizations: Status distribution (pie), Priority breakdown (donut), Department comparison (bar), Monthly trend (area), Project health scatter plot.

AI Intelligence
The AI Agents Behind
Orbit PM
7 purpose-built autonomous agents that analyze, score, recommend, and report.
🤖
Portfolio Health Analyst
Agent 01
Scans 142+ projects simultaneously. Computes overall health score (0-100). Generates department grades (A-F). Produces targeted actionable recommendations for specific at-risk projects.
📊
CCO Decision Matrix
Agent 02
Auto-triages entire portfolio into 3 buckets: Escalated (Delayed + Critical), Attention (slipping timelines), Watch (on track). Replaces 2-3 days of manual governance prep.
⚠️
Risk Intelligence Engine
Agent 03
Monitors 12 risk categories. Auto-scores: Impact × Likelihood = Score (1-9). Generates 3×3 heatmap. Tracks Open vs Closed velocity. Auto-severity classification with color-coded badges.
💬
NLQ Chat Assistant
Agent 04
Natural language querying of portfolio data. "Which projects are delayed?" "How is Engineering?" "Who is overloaded?" Returns formatted, data-driven responses with suggested query chips.
📄
Report Generator
Agent 05
Generates 4 report types: Executive Summary, Department Deep-Dive, Risk Assessment, Full Portfolio. Multi-sheet .xlsx output with KPI dashboards and per-department breakdowns.
👥
Resource & Workload Monitor
Agent 06
Aggregates per-owner project assignments. Traffic light system: 🔴 Overloaded (15+) · 🟡 At Capacity (10-14) · 🟢 Healthy (<10). Prevents burnout, flags rebalancing needs.
